# Writing the deck

Write the way you would explain the point to a smart friend. Full sentences. Everyday words. One idea at a time.

## Do this

- Say what the slide is for in the title. "Decide the job of the slide" is a title. "Synergy" is not.
- Make each bullet a bold label, a colon, and one sentence of about 10 to 20 words.
- Keep three or four bullets. The fifth idea is the next slide.
- Put timing, asides, sources, and extra detail in the speaker notes.
- Use 0 to 2 numbers on an analysis slide. Put a single big figure on its own slide with a label and a caption.
- Keep a fact identical everywhere: same name, same figure, same spelling. If a number changes, change it on every slide and in the source line.

## Avoid

- Flourish, slogans, and stacked clauses joined by semicolons.
- The pattern "X, but Y" used over and over.
- Decorative quotation marks. A quote slide is for someone's real words, with their name.
- Cutting words so the type can shrink. Split the slide instead.

Bad: "Unlock the next horizon; we align, we accelerate, we win, but we stay human."

Good: "Each slide makes one point a listener can repeat."

Bad bullet: "Impact."

Good bullet: **One job:** Say the single thing this slide is for.

## Numbers

A number on a slide needs a source you could open. If you do not have one, do not type the number. "About half" is still a claim. Either source it or drop it.

The big-number layout is for one figure. The label says what it measures. The caption says what it means and where it came from. An analysis slide next to it may mention that figure again, and at most one other number.

## Vietnamese

Write Vietnamese with full diacritics. Do not strip marks to make a font fit. Use Be Vietnam Pro (or another face you have checked) and render the slide before you send it.

Bad: "Toi uu hoa synergy de but pha moi gioi han!"

Good: "Mỗi buổi cần một việc cụ thể và một người chịu trách nhiệm."

The bad line drops the marks and borrows a slogan. The good line is one plain sentence: each meeting needs one concrete task and one person who owns it.

## Consistency check before you send

Read the deck once for the story and once for the facts.

- The same person, place, and product have the same name on every slide.
- A figure in the middle of the deck matches the figure on the data slide and the link on the sources slide.
- The title and the bullets agree. The notes do not contradict the slide.
- English and Vietnamese say the same thing when both appear.
